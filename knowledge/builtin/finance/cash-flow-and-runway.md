# Cash flow, burn and runway

## Cash is not profit
A company can be profitable on an accrual basis and still run out of cash, because revenue may be collected later than costs are paid and because investments and debt repayments do not appear in the income statement. Cash management therefore needs its own forecasting and controls.

## Burn rate and runway
Gross burn is total cash spent per month. Net burn is cash spent minus cash received. Runway is current cash divided by average net burn, expressed in months. Many investors expect venture-backed companies to start raising capital with at least 9 to 12 months of runway remaining, because fundraising often takes longer than planned.

## The 13-week cash flow forecast
A 13-week rolling forecast projects weekly cash receipts and disbursements by category: customer collections, payroll, suppliers, rent, taxes, debt service and capital expenditures. It is updated every week and compared with actuals. It is the standard tool in tight liquidity situations and a useful early-warning system in normal times.

## Working capital
Working capital ties up cash in receivables and inventory and releases it through payables. Days sales outstanding (DSO), days inventory outstanding (DIO) and days payables outstanding (DPO) combine into the cash conversion cycle: DSO plus DIO minus DPO. Shortening the cycle, for example by invoicing promptly, enforcing payment terms or reducing excess inventory, frees cash without new financing.

## Extending runway
Options include reducing discretionary spending, slowing hiring, renegotiating supplier terms, collecting receivables faster, shifting customers to annual prepayment, using non-dilutive financing such as revenue-based financing or venture debt, and prioritizing initiatives with the fastest cash return. Cuts should be made once and deeply enough rather than repeatedly, which damages morale.

## Scenario planning for cash
Model a base case, a downside case and a severe case. Define in advance which actions will be taken if cash falls below specific thresholds, so decisions are not made under panic.
