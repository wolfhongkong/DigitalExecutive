# Commercial contract essentials

## Scope of this note
This is general business orientation, not legal advice. Contract law differs by jurisdiction and by the facts of each deal. Material contracts should be reviewed by qualified counsel.

## Key commercial terms
Every commercial contract should clearly define the parties, the scope of products or services, deliverables and acceptance criteria, price and payment terms, term and renewal, and service levels where relevant. Ambiguity in scope is the most common source of disputes; specify what is excluded as well as what is included.

## Risk allocation clauses
Limitation of liability caps the amount a party can recover, often linked to fees paid over a period, and typically excludes indirect or consequential losses. Common carve-outs from the cap include breach of confidentiality, data protection breaches, intellectual property infringement indemnities, fraud and gross negligence. Indemnities allocate responsibility for specific third-party claims. Warranties are promises about the state of products or services. Insurance requirements back these obligations.

## Termination
Contracts should specify termination for convenience (whether allowed and with what notice), termination for cause (material breach, with a cure period), insolvency, and the consequences of termination such as data return, transition assistance and survival of key clauses.

## Confidentiality and data
Define confidential information, permitted use and duration of obligations. Where personal data is processed on behalf of another party, data protection law often requires a specific data processing agreement covering purpose, security measures, sub-processors and cross-border transfers.

## Governing law and disputes
Choose the governing law and the forum for disputes, such as courts in a named jurisdiction or arbitration. Escalation clauses that require management discussion before litigation can resolve many disputes cheaply.

## Contract management
Maintain a contract repository with owners, renewal and notice dates, key obligations and approval records. Use templates and a playbook of acceptable fallback positions so that routine contracts can be negotiated quickly and consistently.
