# Go-to-market strategy

## What a go-to-market strategy covers
A go-to-market (GTM) strategy defines who the target customers are, what problem the offering solves for them, how it is positioned against alternatives, how it is priced and packaged, and through which channels and motions it reaches and converts customers. It connects product, marketing, sales and customer success.

## Ideal customer profile and segmentation
An ideal customer profile describes the companies or consumers who get the most value and are most valuable to serve: firmographics or demographics, needs, buying triggers and constraints. Buyer personas describe the people involved in the purchase, including users, economic buyers and influencers. Focus on a narrow beachhead segment before expanding.

## Choosing a go-to-market motion
Product-led growth relies on users adopting the product directly, often through free trials or freemium, and suits low-friction products with fast time to value. Sales-led growth uses inside or field sales for higher-value, more complex purchases. Partner-led motions use resellers, marketplaces or integrators. Many companies combine motions by segment. Average contract value usually determines which motions are economically viable.

## Channels
Evaluate channels by reach within the target segment, cost per acquired customer and scalability: search and content marketing, paid social, events, outbound sales, partnerships, communities and referrals. Test several channels with small budgets, measure customer acquisition cost and conversion, then concentrate on the few that work.

## Launch planning
A launch plan defines goals and metrics, target audience, messaging, enablement for sales and support, launch activities and timeline, and a post-launch review. Tier launches by importance so that major launches receive full cross-functional effort and minor updates do not.

## Measuring GTM performance
Track funnel conversion from awareness to customer, customer acquisition cost, sales cycle length, win rate, activation and retention. Review the funnel by segment and channel to find bottlenecks.
