# Sales pipeline and qualification

## Pipeline stages
A sales pipeline divides the buying journey into stages with clear entry and exit criteria, for example: qualified lead, discovery completed, solution validated, proposal sent, negotiation, closed won or lost. Criteria should describe buyer actions, such as agreeing to a technical evaluation, rather than seller activities such as sending an email.

## Qualification frameworks
BANT checks Budget, Authority, Need and Timing, and suits simpler transactional sales. MEDDIC and its extension MEDDICC check Metrics (quantified value), Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Identify pain, Champion and, in MEDDICC, Competition. These frameworks suit complex enterprise sales with several stakeholders. Disqualifying poor-fit opportunities early is as valuable as advancing good ones.

## Discovery
Good discovery uncovers the customer's current situation, the business problem and its cost, the desired outcome, who is affected, what has been tried, and why the problem matters now. Ask open questions, listen more than you talk and confirm understanding in writing. Without a compelling reason to act now, deals tend to stall.

## Pipeline health metrics
Track pipeline coverage (pipeline value relative to quota, often three to four times for the period depending on win rate), stage conversion rates, average deal size, sales cycle length, win rate and pipeline velocity: number of opportunities times average deal size times win rate, divided by sales cycle length.

## Pipeline reviews
Hold regular pipeline reviews focused on specific deals and next steps rather than updating fields. Look for deals stuck in a stage, missing next steps, missing access to the economic buyer and close dates that keep moving. Clean the pipeline of stale opportunities so that forecasts reflect reality.

## Founder-led sales
In early companies, founders often sell first. They should document what they learn about buyers, objections and the sales process before hiring sales staff, so the first hires inherit a repeatable playbook.
