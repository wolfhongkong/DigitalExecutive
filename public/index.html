      Identify speakers
      
      
      The AI analyzes speech across the recording using voice characteristics and time-aligned video frames, then presents anonymous speaker cards. Connect each speaker to an existing executive once to assign all matching turns. Listen to the sample and check the suggested visual cue. Video is sampled, not continuously watched; AI associations can be wrong. Off-screen, uncertain or mixed speech may have no visual match or stay unassigned. Executive identities are supplied by you, never inferred by the AI.

Closing retains the analysis; save a draft to keep unfinished assignments.
